# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Nell to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless
of background or identity.

## Our standards

Be respectful, assume good faith, and give constructive feedback. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private
information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at the contact address listed in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove content or contributors that violate this code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
